Tendril

A few rewriting rules, applied again and again, then read aloud to a turtle with a pen. Pick a plant or a curve, edit its grammar, and watch it grow.

Keys: G replays the growth, N draws a new seed, [ and ] step the generation down and up.

Turtle commands: drawing letters step with the pen down, f steps with it up, + and - turn, | turns around, [ and ] save and restore the turtle, ! narrows the pen, " shortens the step. Plant presets follow chapter 1 of Prusinkiewicz and Lindenmayer, The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants.
